## The entry

One out-of-office auto-reply, written as if a houseplant sent it:

> Out of office. I haven't been watered since March and I'm thriving. Your email will be ignored in the same spirit.

`main.py` (pure stdlib) writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` — 114 characters, inside the 120-character gate.
